B · Card picked choose a column

  • Click a hand card: it lifts, gold ringed; every slot turns dashed gold
  • Click a slot to lay the card there; click the card again to drop it
  • Or drag the card onto a slot: slots turn dashed gold as the drag starts, the one under the card gets a gold ring
  • Replaces today's column buttons in the banner prompt

C · Two staged confirm

  • A staged card leaves the hand and shows in its slot; click it there to take it back
  • At most one bird: once one is staged, the other bird cards go dark
  • Two staged: the rest of the hand goes dark, Confirm sends both
